Privacy Policy — Discipline Tracker

Your data stays yours. This plain-language policy explains what Discipline Tracker collects, why, and how you stay in control. It applies to the Discipline Tracker app on Android and iOS.

In short: We only store what you create in the app. We don’t sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We show no ads and use no third-party ad or analytics SDKs. Analytics and insights are computed on your device.

04How we use information

  • Run the app: calculate streaks, insights, reminders — all on your device.
  • Back up your data to your own Google Drive (hidden app folder discipline-backup.json) so you don’t lose progress.
  • Verify subscriptions and unlock Pro features.
  • Fix bugs and keep the app secure.

We will never use your logs to show you ads.

05Where your data lives

Two places: (1) on your device (SQLite / secure storage for fast offline access), and (2) in your own Google Drive app-data folder if you enable backup. Drive transfers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) by Google. Only you can access it with your Google account — we operate no general-purpose app servers that store your habit logs.

06Subscription / purchase verification

If you purchase a subscription, Google Play shares the product ID and purchase token with the app. The app sends that token to our verification endpoint (hosted on Firebase in us-central1), which checks with the Google Play Developer API whether the subscription is active, acknowledged, and unexpired, then returns only a yes/no + expiry date. The token is not used for any other purpose and is not linked to your habit logs.

07What we share (and don’t)

We don’t sell your data. Ever. Your personal information is not sold, rented, or traded.
  • Service providers (only to run the app): Google (Sign-In, Drive storage in your account, Play Billing verification). See Google Privacy Policy and Google Play payments.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect safety and prevent abuse.
  • With your consent: otherwise we ask first.

No ad networks, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking.

08How long we keep data

  • On-device data is kept until you use “Clear all data” or uninstall.
  • Drive backup is kept until you delete it in Settings → Delete Drive backup. Deleted Drive files stay in Google Drive trash until you empty it at drive.google.com/drive/trash (per Google’s policy).
  • Purchase verification logs (token validity, expiry) are kept only as long as needed to prevent fraud and support subscriptions, then discarded.

09Your choices & rights (including deletion)

  • Access & edit: view/change profile, habits, logs anytime in the app.
  • Delete cloud copy: Settings → Delete Drive backup, then empty Drive trash.
  • Delete device copy: Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall.
  • Revoke Google access: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove Discipline Tracker (stops future backups).
  • Notifications: Settings in-app or Android system settings.
  • GDPR / CCPA: EU/UK users have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object. California users have rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale (we do not sell). Email support@disciplinetracker.app to exercise any right.

Account deletion: deleting your Drive backup + clearing app data + revoking Google access fully removes your data from our scope. Google retains data per its own policy.

10Children

Discipline Tracker is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), OS secure storage / Keychain-Keystore, and least-privilege Drive scope (drive.appdata — only the app’s hidden folder, not your whole Drive). No system is 100% secure; we will notify you of any serious breach affecting your data.

12International transfers

If you enable Google backup or subscribe, data may be processed by Google / Firebase in the US or other regions under Google’s safeguards. Your on-device data never leaves your phone unless you enable backup or verification.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app improves. We will change the date at the top and, for material changes, notify you in the app. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the new policy. This version: v1.0 – May 1, 2026.
